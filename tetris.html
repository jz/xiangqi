Tetris Duel

First to top out loses. Clearing 2, 3, or 4 lines at once sends 1, 2, or 4 garbage rows to your opponent — they land when their current piece locks. Watch the red Incoming meter. Each player can pause the match once per game — Q for Player 1, P for Player 2 — for up to 30 seconds; the same key resumes, and after 10 seconds the other player's pause key can resume too. Turn on Same blocks (B) to give both players the identical piece sequence, or set Opponent (C) to Computer to play solo against the machine.
